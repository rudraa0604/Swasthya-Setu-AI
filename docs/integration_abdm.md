# Integration Guide: ABDM (Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission)

## 1. Overview
ABDM provides a federated architecture for India's digital health ecosystem. SwasthyaSetu AI aligns with ABDM standards:
- **FHIR R4 / NDHM Compliance**: Facility and medication resource structures align with ABDM HFR (Health Facility Registry) and standard medication nomenclature.
- **HIP / HIU Role**: Operates as a Health Information User for aggregated consumption analytics, preserving strict patient anonymity.
- **ABHA Compatibility**: Does not store individual ABHA IDs; processes de-identified cohort telemetry to preserve citizen privacy.
